SEVRR Support

Email support@sevrr.app. We reply within two working days.

If you are in crisis

SEVRR is not an emergency service. If you are thinking about harming yourself, contact local emergency services or a crisis line now. In the US, call or text 988.

Turning the blocker off

Open SEVRR and tap "I want to turn it off". A 72-hour wait starts; you can cancel it any time. When it ends you have two hours to finish three puzzles, a breathing timer, a check-in and your recorded message. The blocker then stays off for 24 hours and turns itself back on.

Cancelling your subscription

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then SEVRR. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel the subscription.

Deleting your account

Open SEVRR, then Settings, then Delete account. Your data is deleted immediately. If your blocker was on, your iPhone stays blocked for 72 hours and then SEVRR turns it off.

Lock it down passcode

SEVRR shows your Screen Time passcode only at the end of the 72-hour flow, 72 hours after your subscription ends, or 72 hours after you delete your account. Support cannot reveal it any other way. As a last resort, Apple lets the device owner reset a forgotten Screen Time passcode with their Apple Account.

Reporting content or a user

Use the "..." menu on any post, comment or partner screen. Reports are reviewed by a person.
