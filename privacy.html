SEVRR Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

SEVRR is a blocker for adult content that is deliberately hard to turn off. Because of what SEVRR is for, everything you share with it is sensitive. This policy explains exactly what SEVRR collects, why, who else sees it, and how to delete it. "SEVRR", "we" and "us" mean the developer of the SEVRR app, listed as the seller on the App Store.

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your phone

  • Your browsing. Blocking runs inside Apple's Screen Time on your iPhone. Apple does not give SEVRR your browsing history, and SEVRR never sees which sites you visit or try to visit.
  • Apps you choose to block. Apple gives SEVRR only anonymous tokens for the apps you pick. We cannot see their names.
  • Your quiz answers from onboarding.
  • Your unlock check-in (the "what's happening right now?" answer during the 72-hour flow).

Analytics

If analytics is on, SEVRR sends a small, fixed list of anonymous events: which onboarding screens were viewed, paywall views and purchases, activation, and which main features were opened. They are tied to a random identifier created on your phone, not to your account or email. SEVRR never sends relapses, notes, quiz answers, check-ins, usernames, post text or anything about your partner. There is no session recording, no automatic screen capture and no advertising identifier. You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings.

Community content and moderation

Posts and comments are visible to other signed-in SEVRR users. Profiles are not searchable. Before a post or comment appears, its text is screened automatically (rules first, then Anthropic's model). Items the screen cannot clear are held for a person on our team to review. We keep a record of moderation decisions and reports to handle appeals and abuse. If a post suggests someone may be at risk of self-harm, we show support resources.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account:

  • Your account and everything linked to it (profile, streak, history, partner pairing, alerts, posts, comments, reports, voice backup, push tokens) are deleted immediately.
  • If your blocker was on, or you used Lock it down, we keep one record that is not linked to your identity: the time your blocker may turn off (72 hours later) and your encrypted Screen Time passcode, if any. Your phone uses it to turn the blocker off and show the passcode. It is deleted when your phone has collected it, and in any case within 30 days.
  • Our hosting provider keeps encrypted backups for a short period (up to 7 days) before they are overwritten.
  • Apple keeps its own records of your purchases under Apple's policies.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings, then Delete account.
  • Turn off the voice backup and delete the stored copy in Settings.
  • Turn off analytics in Settings.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or to correct it, by emailing support@sevrr.app. We reply within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have further rights, including to object to processing or to complain to a data-protection authority. We process your data to provide the service you asked for (contract), to keep the community safe (legitimate interests), and, for analytics, based on your choice, which you can withdraw in Settings.

Age

SEVRR is only for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor is using SEVRR, contact us and we will delete the account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Server functions decide anything time-sensitive, and clients can only read their own private data. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it applies.
