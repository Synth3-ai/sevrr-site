01 · The blocker

It blocks at the system level.

SEVRR uses Apple's Screen Time to block adult sites and apps across your whole iPhone, not just one browser. And turning it off is slow on purpose.

  • Adult sites blocked in every browser
  • You can't delete apps while it's on
  • Turning it off starts a 72-hour wait. Cancel any time, free

02 · Weak moment

One tap when it hits.

The red Weak moment button is always one tap away. It plays the message you recorded to your future self and walks you through a minute of breathing.

  • Your own voice, recorded when you were clear-headed
  • Guided breathing, one minute
  • Time for the urge to pass

03 · Progress

A streak you can see.

Count your days and earn a new core at every milestone, 30 in all, up to day 100 and beyond. A relapse ends the streak, not your progress: your longest run and earned milestones stay.

  • Live streak, chart and day grid
  • A new core at every milestone
  • Honest about slips, never shaming

04 · Together

You're not doing this alone.

Optionally, an accountability partner is alerted when you ask to turn it off. No approval needed from them, and the app works fully without one. A moderated community is there too.

  • A partner matched to you, in the app
  • Alerts when you ask to turn it off
  • A community of people on the same road

The 72-hour wait

Turning it off is slow on purpose.

01

Ask

Start the wait. Your blocker stays on. Your partner, if you have one, is told.

02

Wait 72 hours

A long pause, on purpose, so you can change your mind. Cancel the wait any time, free.

03

Slow down

Three puzzles, a breathing timer, a check-in, and the message you recorded to yourself.

04

Back on

The blocker turns itself back on after 24 hours.
