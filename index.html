How it works

It blocks at the system level

SEVRR uses Apple's Screen Time to block adult sites and apps across your whole iPhone, not just one browser.

Turning it off is slow on purpose

You ask, then wait 72 hours. At the end: three puzzles, a breathing timer, a check-in, and a message you recorded to your future self. You can cancel any time, free.

A partner who's told

Optionally, an accountability partner is alerted when you ask to turn it off. No approval needed from them, and the app works fully without one.

A streak you can see

Count your days and earn milestones. A relapse ends the streak, not your progress: your longest run and earned milestones stay.
